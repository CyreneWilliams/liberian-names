How we use contributions

Liberian Names is an open, community-built record of Liberian traditional names, cared for by Cyrene Williams and Winifred Gray-Johnson. Everything here was shared by people who know these names, and we want you to know what happens to what you share.

What happens when you contribute

An editor reads every meaning, spelling, story and recording before it appears. We may correct spelling or shorten text, but we won't change what you said a name means.

How it's shown

Approved contributions appear on the name's page, with the name you gave for credit, or none if you left it blank. Recordings are published for anyone to listen to. We never show your email address.

How it's kept and shared

The archive is a cultural record, so contributions are kept for the long term. Researchers may download and cite a yearly copy of the archive. We may also share it with universities, libraries and heritage archives that help preserve it. We do not sell contributions or use them for advertising.

Names that carry special meaning

Some names belong to societies, ceremonies or families. If you believe a name or story should not be public, tell us and we'll take advice from elders.

Removing something

You can ask us to remove your contribution or recording at any time. Email info@liberiannames.com with the name it's attached to, and we'll remove it within 14 days. Copies already downloaded by researchers can't be recalled, but they will not appear in later releases.
