      About the archive

      Where the names come from

      In 2020 Liberians at home and abroad were asked for the traditional names they knew, what they mean, and which tribe uses them. Repeat answers were merged, so each name appears once with every meaning people gave and a count of how many people shared it.

      About spelling

      Most of these names were spoken long before they were written, so families spell them differently. Decontee, Dekonti and Dehkontee are all here, and search finds every spelling we have.

      Who's behind it

      Liberian Names was founded by Cyrene Williams and Winifred Gray-Johnson. Both are co-founders and contributors: they started gathering names in 2020 and still add to the archive.
